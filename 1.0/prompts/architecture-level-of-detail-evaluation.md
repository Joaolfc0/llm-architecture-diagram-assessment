### Evaluation Criterion — Level of Detail

Level of detail is about whether the elements of the diagram are presented with an appropriate amount of detail for understanding the architecture.

In particular, evaluate:

1. **Element Detail**  
   - Are components described with enough information (names, roles, responsibilities) to understand what they do?

2. **Interaction Detail**  
   - Are interactions shown with sufficient detail (direction and type of communication, such as request/response or messaging) to understand how the system behaves?

3. **Overall Appropriateness for Developers and Architects**  
   - Considering developers and architects as the audience, is the level of detail balanced (neither too superficial nor excessively detailed for the architectural view)?

---

### Rating Scale (Level of Detail)

- **Exemplary Level of Detail**: Excellent balance; elements and interactions are presented with a level of detail that is well-suited for developers and architects.
- **Acceptable Level of Detail**: Generally appropriate, with minor parts that are slightly over-detailed or under-detailed.
- **Insufficient Level of Detail**: Clear mismatch between what the audience needs and what the diagram shows (too vague or too detailed in important areas).
- **Deficient Level of Detail**: Strongly misaligned with the audience’s needs; the diagram is almost empty or overly abstract, or overloaded with low-level details that obscure the architecture.
