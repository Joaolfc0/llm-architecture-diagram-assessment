### Evaluation Criteria — Level of Detail
Evaluate whether the level of detail is appropriate for the target audience. Technical diagrams may require more details for developers, while high-level diagrams for stakeholders should simplify complex concepts. If the diagram is too detailed or too vague for the intended audience, suggest changes to meet the audience's needs.

### Rating Scale
- **Exemplary Level of Detail**: The diagram and description present an ideal balance of abstraction and specificity for the intended audience. Technical precision and conceptual clarity are harmonized, ensuring accessibility without oversimplification. The representation demonstrates clear awareness of audience needs and communicative intent.
- **Acceptable Level of Detail**: The materials are generally well-calibrated to the target audience, though some portions may be slightly over- or under-detailed. These minor adjustments would enhance alignment with audience expectations but do not substantially hinder comprehension.
- **Insufficient Level of Detail**: The diagram and description deviate noticeably from the audience's informational needs. Either excessive technical complexity or excessive abstraction obscures understanding. A moderate rebalancing of detail and focus is required to improve appropriateness.
- **Deficient Level of Detail**: The level of detail is wholly misaligned with the target audience. The content either overwhelms with unnecessary technicality or lacks sufficient specificity to convey the architecture meaningfully. Major restructuring is needed to tailor the materials effectively.
