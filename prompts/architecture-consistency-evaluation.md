### Rating Scale
- **Exemplary Consistency**: The diagram and description exhibit complete uniformity in the use of symbols, styles, and terminology. Visual and textual representations are fully aligned, reinforcing a coherent understanding of the architecture. No inconsistencies are present, and the design demonstrates high methodological discipline.
- **Acceptable Consistency**: The materials are largely consistent, with only minor deviations in notation, terminology, or stylistic alignment between the diagram and the description. These do not significantly impair comprehension but should be corrected for full standardization.
- **Insufficient Consistency**: Several inconsistencies exist in the representation of components, naming conventions, or descriptions. These discrepancies reduce clarity and may cause misinterpretation of the system's structure or relationships. Revisions are needed to harmonize symbols, styles, and textual elements.
- **Deficient Consistency**: The diagram and description demonstrate widespread inconsistency in symbols, terminology, or design language. The misalignment between visual and textual representations severely undermines interpretability and credibility. Comprehensive redesign and terminology standardization are required.
