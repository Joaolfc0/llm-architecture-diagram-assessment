### Rating Scale
- **Exemplary Clarity**: The diagram and description are exceptionally clear and informative. They enable comprehension without ambiguity and demonstrate deliberate attention to accessibility for diverse audiences. Only negligible stylistic refinements could be considered.
- **Acceptable Clarity**: The materials communicate their intent effectively overall but exhibit minor issues (e.g., inconsistent labeling, slight visual clutter, or modest terminology mismatches). These do not impede understanding but merit targeted improvement.
- **Insufficient Clarity**: The materials convey partial understanding but contain multiple weaknesses that hinder interpretability (e.g., unclear symbol semantics, poor flow depiction, or inconsistent textual explanations). Substantial revision is required.
- **Deficient Clarity**: The diagram and/or description fail to communicate the architecture coherently. Key elements are missing, mislabeled, or confusing, leading to serious misinterpretation. Major restructuring and rewriting are necessary.
